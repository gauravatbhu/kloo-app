## Watch list (re-check monthly)
- Any competitor adds Hindi or Hinglish, or a fully on-device answer step. Either would weaken our two lead claims.
- SavedThat launches on iOS. Stashr launches a mobile app.
- Any app adds INR pricing or a Google Play downloads band above 10K.
- Platform enforcement against session-based import or scraping (Meta, TikTok), and how these apps respond.
